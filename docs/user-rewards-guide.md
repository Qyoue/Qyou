# How Queue Rewards Work: User Guide & FAQ

Welcome to Qyou Rewards! Qyou allows queue participants to earn real on-chain cryptocurrency rewards (Stellar Lumens, or XLM) simply by joining, waiting, and successfully completing eligible queues.

---

## 🌟 How It Works in 3 Simple Steps

```
[ 1. Link Your Wallet ] ──► [ 2. Join & Complete a Queue ] ──► [ 3. Receive XLM On-Chain ]
```

1. **Set Up or Connect a Wallet**:
   - Go to **Settings > Wallet** or connect during onboarding.
   - Choose **Option A (Automated Wallet)** if you don't already have crypto experience—Qyou handles key generation automatically!
   - Choose **Option B (Connect Existing Wallet)** if you use Freighter, Albedo, Lobstr, or a hardware wallet.
2. **Join an Incentive-Enabled Queue**:
   - Look for the 🪙 **Reward Badge** on queues in your feed. The badge displays the payout amount (for example, `+5.0 XLM`).
   - Remain in the queue until your position is reached and your turn is marked complete by the queue operator.
3. **Automatic Payout**:
   - As soon as your queue completion is verified, our automated Soroban smart contract dispatches your reward directly to your linked Stellar address.
   - You will see a live confirmation notification with a link to inspect the transaction on the Stellar blockchain explorer.

## 📋 Reward Eligibility Rules (#1054)

To keep reward pools fair and protect against fraudulent farming, the following rules apply:

### 1. Active Connected Wallet
- You must have a linked, valid Stellar address on file **before or at the time of queue completion**.
- If your wallet is unlinked when you complete a queue, the reward is deferred until you reconnect.

### 2. Verified Queue Completion
- You must actively progress through the queue and have your completion confirmed by the queue host or automated terminal.
- Abandoning, cancelling, or timing out from a queue forfeits that queue's incentive reward.

### 3. Fair-Play Anti-Farming Limits
To ensure that all community members have access to reward pools:
- **One Wallet per Account**: A single Stellar public key cannot be linked to multiple Qyou accounts simultaneously.
- **Per-User Queue Caps**: Certain high-demand queues enforce a maximum number of claims per person (e.g. 1 reward per day per queue).
- **Daily Volume Ceilings**: System-wide daily velocity limits prevent automated bots from draining incentive pools.

## 💡 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is XLM?
XLM (Stellar Lumens) is the native digital currency of the Stellar blockchain network. XLM is fast, energy-efficient, and carries near-zero transaction fees. You can hold your XLM, swap it for other assets, or transfer it to any crypto exchange.

### Do I have to pay any network gas or transaction fees?
**No.** All blockchain network fees for sending rewards are covered directly by Qyou. You receive 100% of the advertised reward amount.

### How fast do rewards arrive?
Stellar ledgers settle in approximately 3 to 5 seconds. Payouts typically appear in your wallet almost instantaneously upon queue completion.

### Can I change or unlink my wallet?
Yes! You can disconnect your wallet at any time under **Settings > Wallet > Unlink Wallet**. For your protection, unlinking requires confirming your account password or session authentication.

### Where can I check the status of my payout?
You can view your historical reward payouts in your Qyou account dashboard, or click the transaction link on any confirmed reward to view the cryptographic proof on [Stellar Expert](https://stellar.expert).
